        Etymon

        Words wander. Nice once meant foolish; silly meant blessed; a girl could be a boy. You are one of these words, at the start of its journey.

        The map is meaning. Each dot is a sense, and your circle holds the senses you carry. The little dots inside it are your speakers: the people who say you. Each century:

        1. 1Read the clue. Each century wants words for two meanings, and the clue hints which. You only find out afterwards.
        2. 2Tap a meaning on the map to drift your word toward it. You can only drift as far as the dashed ring.
        3. 3Optional: broaden a lot, or narrow a little. Broad is a safer guess, but grow too big and it takes centuries to shrink.
        4. 4Let the century pass. New speakers walk in from each meaning you hold, far more from the wanted ones; some forget you.
