      Etymon

      You are a word. Over a thousand years, win speakers by meaning what each century wants, and find out what you become.

      1. 1Choose what happens, and lock it in. One event is what really happened; the other changes history. Guess which: you find out afterwards. The event sets the century's tastes: two meanings it wants and two out of fashion, each hinted at by a clue.
      2. 2Drift. Tap the map to move your circle, as far as the dashed ring. Tap + or − on it to broaden a lot or narrow a little. What's inside is what you mean. Zoom with the buttons in the corner, a pinch or the wheel; drag from outside your reach to look around.
      3. 3Continue. See what the century did to your speakers, then choose what happens next.
      • Each meaning you carry wins +3, or +30 if wanted. One out of fashion costs −25.
      • Over three meanings and you're vague: what you win is diluted. None costs −20. Every century 10 speakers forget you.
      • Fads (✦) sprout beside meanings you've carried: +12 at first, out of fashion two centuries later.
      • Keep history in 3 of the first 5 centuries and you become the real word; change it more and you become the world you've changed it to. With three centuries left you learn which: carry it at the end for +100.
      • Tap a century on the timeline to see what it did. ✓ means you kept to history.
