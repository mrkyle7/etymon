      Etymon

      Words wander. Nice once meant foolish; silly meant blessed; a girl could be a boy. You are one of these words, at the start of its journey, and what you mean today depends on where you go.

      The map is meaning. Each dot is a meaning, and your circle holds the ones you carry. The little dots inside it are your speakers. Each century:

      1. 1Read the clue. Each century wants words for two meanings, and the clue hints which. What it wants depends on where your word has got to, so the clues follow your path. You find out afterwards.
      2. 2Tap a meaning to drift toward it, as far as the dashed ring.
      3. 3Optional: broaden a lot, or narrow a little. Broad is a safer guess, but it takes centuries to shrink again.
      4. 4Let the century pass. Speakers walk in from each meaning you carry, far more from wanted ones; some forget you.

      • Every meaning you carry wins +3 speakers, or +18 if the century wanted it.
      • Out of fashion meanings (red ✕) cost 15 each while you carry them that century.
      • Fads (✦) sprout beside meanings you've carried. They pay +8 the century they appear, and go out of fashion two centuries later.
      • The panel shows what you mean now, the clue for next, and what would happen if you drift where your circle is. ✓ under a century means you meant what the word really meant then.
      • Carry more than three and you're too vague (−10 each extra); none and nobody knows what you mean (−20). Every century, 10 people forget you anyway.
      • What you become is up to you. After each century you learn what the word really meant then. Mean that in three of the first five centuries and you'll become what it means today. Stray, and your word finds another ending. You'll learn which with three centuries to go: carry it at the end for +100.

      At the end, see how your journey differed from the word's real one, and how close you came to the perfect route to your ending.
